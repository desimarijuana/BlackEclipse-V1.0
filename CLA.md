# Contributor Licence Agreement (CLA)

Version 1.0 — 2026-11-17

This is a plain-English Contributor Licence Agreement between you ("you" or
"Contributor") and **Rafay Ghauri**, maintainer of the BlackEclipse project
("the Project"). It covers every contribution you submit to this repository.

> **Why this exists.** The DCO (see [`Black-Eclipse-site/DCO`](Black-Eclipse-site/DCO))
> says you have the right to submit your work. It does **not** give the Project
> the right to relicense it. This CLA does. Without it, one merged pull request
> would permanently lock the Project to MIT and rule out a future commercial
> edition, dual licence, or licence change. Signing costs you nothing you would
> not already give under MIT.

You remain the owner of your contribution. Nothing here transfers ownership away
from you.

irrevocable** licence to:

- reproduce, modify, and distribute your contribution;
- sublicense it to others; and
- **relicense it under any licence terms the Project chooses**, including
  licences different from the one covering the repository today.

This is what keeps the Project free to change its licence, publish a commercial
edition, or dual-license in the future. The Project's own published licence
always applies to the code as distributed — this clause only decides who *may*
choose it.

You also grant a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable
patent licence covering patent claims you own or control that your contribution
necessarily infringes.

## 3. What you confirm

You confirm that:

1. each contribution is your original work, or you have the right to submit it;
2. you are legally able to grant the licences above (for example, your employer
   has agreed, or no employer claim applies);
3. it contains no third-party code under incompatible terms;
4. it contains no secrets, credentials, or personal data.

If an employer holds rights to work you create, you confirm you have permission
to contribute it under this CLA.

## 4. What you get

Your contribution stays available to everyone under the Project's current
licence — today MIT for the application, AGPL-3.0-only for
`Black-Eclipse-site/pine-engine/`. You are credited in the repository history,
and the CLA gives you no obligation to provide support.

## 5. No warranty

Contributions are provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, to the extent
permitted by law.

## 6. Scope and termination

This CLA covers contributions submitted while it is in force. The licence you
grant in §2 is irrevocable. A future CLA version applies only to contributions
made after it takes effect.

## 7. How to sign

There is no separate signature portal. In your first pull request, add this
comment:

```
I have read CLA.md and I agree to it for my contributions to this repository.
```

That comment, together with your `Signed-off-by:` commit lines (DCO), is your
signature. Keep signing off every commit:

```sh
git commit -s -m "your message"
```

Small fixes to documentation may be merged without the CLA comment at the
maintainer's discretion; code contributions require it.

## 8. Corporate contributors

If you contribute on behalf of a company, an authorised representative must post
the same comment with their name and the company name. The Project may request a
signed copy.
