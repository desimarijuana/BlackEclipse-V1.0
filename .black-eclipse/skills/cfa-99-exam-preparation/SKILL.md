# CFA Level I — Exam Preparation

Cross-cutting preparation for the whole exam. Topic content lives in
`cfa.01` through `cfa.10`; this skill is the process around it.

## The hours rule

CFA Institute's only official figure: successful candidates report **over 300
hours per level**. Everything below is built to clear that with buffer.

| Plan | Weeks | Hours/week | Total | Hours/day (6-day week) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A - aggressive | 13 | 22 | 286 | 3.7 |
| **B - balanced (default)** | **26** | **13** | **338** | **2.2** |
| C - relaxed | 39 | 8.5 | 332 | 1.4 |
| D - very relaxed | 52 | 7 | 364 | 1.2 |

**Plan B is the recommendation for a beginner:** the smallest plan that clears 300
hours with buffer. Plan A has no buffer and assumes prior exposure.

## Phases

0. **Setup** — register, obtain the curriculum, learn the calculator, build the
   tracker, notes, flashcards and formula sheet.
1. **Foundations** — the quantitative prerequisites below.
2. **Core learning** — the ten topics in index order, with cumulative practice
   from the start.
3. **Practice** — raise volume, rank weaknesses by (weight x deficit).
4. **Revision** — formulas, active recall, spaced repetition, Ethics, weak areas.
5. **Mocks** — full timed exams plus analysis and targeted repair.
6. **Final two weeks** — revision only, no new content.
7. **Final 48 hours** — formula sheet and mistake log only; sleep.

## Weekly template (Plan B)

Six study days, one lighter/review day. A 60-75 minute learning block plus a 30-45
minute practice block per weekday, one longer weekend block.

- **Mon-Thu:** new topic block, then 20-30 questions on it.
- **Fri:** light re-read, 15-20 questions, **Ethics session (non-negotiable)**.
- **Sat:** 2-3 hour block — finish the topic, then 40-60 cumulative questions.
- **Sun:** mock or catch-up, plus the weekly review and next-week plan.

## Quantitative prerequisites

Do these **before** Quantitative Methods, in weeks 2-4 of Plan B (about 24 hours).
Every one has a free resource.

| Prerequisite | Why it matters | Free resource |
|---|---|---|
| Arithmetic, order of operations | Every numerical question; sign errors are the commonest lost mark | A free maths course |
| Algebra, simultaneous equations | Solving for rate, periods, present value | Same |
| Exponents and roots | Compounding, effective annual rate, geometric mean | Same |
| **Logarithms** | Solving for the number of periods; continuous compounding | Same |
| **Percentages and percentage change** | Returns and growth rates | Same |
| **Time value of money** | The backbone of Quant, Fixed Income and Equities | A free interest/TVM course |
| Discounting and compounding (periodic, continuous) | Bond pricing, NPV, EAR | Same |
| **Descriptive statistics** | Statistical characteristics module | A free statistics course; an open statistics textbook |
| **Probability** (rules, conditional, Bayes, trees) | Distributions and Bayesian updating | Same |
| **Distributions** (normal, lognormal, binomial, t, chi-square, F) | Directly examined | Same |
| **Sampling and the central limit theorem** | Estimation module | Same |
| **Hypothesis testing** (Type I/II, power, t-tests, ANOVA) | Directly examined | Same |
| **Correlation and covariance** | Portfolio risk and regression | Same |
| **Regression** (OLS, R-squared, residuals, ANOVA) | Simple linear regression module | Same |

**Fluency test, not proof:** if you can solve a TVM problem and a two-variable
hypothesis test unaided, move on.

## Calculator (settle this in week one)

**Only two models are authorised:** the **Texas Instruments BA II Plus**
(including Professional) and the **HP 12C** (including Platinum and anniversary
editions). An unauthorised calculator **voids your results**; borrowing one at the
test centre is prohibited and calculators are inspected.

**Buy the BA II Plus** — cheapest, and what the free tutorials are taught on. Pick
the HP 12C only if you already know reverse Polish notation.

Functions to master: `N I/Y PV PMT FV`; clearing the TVM worksheet; **annuity
mode (end vs beginning)**; periods per year and compounding per year; the **cash
flow worksheet** (NPV/IRR); **interest rate conversion** (nominal/effective); the
**statistics and regression worksheet**; the **bond worksheet**; depreciation;
memory registers.

Sequence (about four hours): read the official calculator guide, set the modes,
then drill TVM (~30 problems), cash flows (~10), rates and statistics (~10). From
then on solve **every** numerical question on the calculator, never mentally.

## Formula strategy

**CFA Institute supplies no formula sheet in the Level I exam.** Plan to know every
formula.

- **Tier 1 — memorise to instant recall:** TVM; holding period return and the
  mean/return measures; portfolio expected return and variance; beta; CAPM; Sharpe,
  Treynor, M-squared, Jensen's alpha; bond price from yield; Macaulay and modified
  duration; PVBP; the duration-plus-convexity price approximation; expected credit
  loss; put-call parity; forward price with cost of carry; the binomial
  probability; WACC; NPV; IRR; the cash conversion cycle; coefficient of
  variation; standard error; t-statistic; the Gordon growth and justified-multiple
  formulas; the alternatives fee computation.
- **Tier 2 — understand and be able to re-derive:** regression outputs; spot/par/
  forward relationships; effective duration and convexity, key rate duration;
  simulation methods; multi-factor model structure; DuPont; the ratio families.
- **Tier 3 — recognise only:** distribution moments; tax-deferral arithmetic;
  securitization tranching detail; **GIPS (not tested at Level I)**.

**Spaced repetition:** review each formula at **day 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 and 60**, then
weekly. Every review must be **active recall** — cover, write, check.

## Question and mock strategy

**Volume targets:** 150-250 questions per major topic, 80-120 per lighter topic;
80-100 per week while learning, 150 per week in the practice phase; **1,000+ before
the first mock**; **2,500-3,000 total** before the final mock. Practice must be
**cumulative** — mix the current topic with earlier ones.

| Mock | When | Purpose | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ~50% through the plan | Diagnosis | Any score; read the breakdown |
| 2 | ~75% through | Improvement | +8-10 points over mock 1 |
| 3 | Practice phase | Timing (finish both sessions) | ~68% |
| 4 | Revision phase | Endurance + Ethics | 70%+ |
| 5 | Final weeks | Exam simulation | 72%+ |
| 6 | Final weeks | Confidence | 73%+ |

**Do not mock before about a third of the curriculum is done.** Always read the
**topic breakdown**, not the headline. Aim for 70%+ before feeling safe.

**Mistake log** — for every wrong answer: question and source; topic; why (knowledge
/ careless / misread / formula); the correct concept in a sentence; the formula;
one action. **Re-solve it seven days later.** Five or more misses in one topic is
not a mistake problem, it is a topic problem — go back and re-learn it.

## Resource strategy (free-first)

- **Official (primary):** the **Learning Ecosystem** — curriculum, topic questions,
  mock exams and flashcards, included with registration and the only material
  guaranteed to match your curriculum year. The **Level I Topic Outlines PDF** and
  the **Code and Standards** and the **calculator guide** are free with no login.
- **Free third-party:** a full free Level I question bank plus written lessons; a
  smaller free question bank with a formula sheet; a free full-length mock with
  worked solutions; a free topic-by-topic video course; **open textbooks** for
  statistics, finance and economics; a free statistics/TVM course for the
  prerequisites.
- **Optional paid:** the official **Practice Pack** (best value), a structured
  video course (only against a demonstrated weakness), a full prep package
  (convenience, not content the free path lacks). **Do not buy in week one.**

**Warnings:** check the **curriculum year** on every third-party resource (2026 or
earlier mis-weights QM and lacks the new modules); never use question "dump" sites
or old edition uploads; a resource that does not state its year is unknown.

## Exam-day protocol (Level I)

180 multiple-choice questions, two 135-minute sessions of 90 each, ~90 seconds per
question, **no penalty for guessing — answer everything**. Bring a valid
**international travel passport** and the approved calculator with spare batteries,
and arrive 30+ minutes early.

**Final two weeks:** no new content; a timed mock every 3-4 days; formulas daily
from memory; Ethics daily; re-do every "hard list" question.

**Final 48 hours — do:** one short practice set; review the formula sheet and
mistake log only; confirm logistics; check the curriculum errata page; sleep and
eat normally.

**Final 48 hours — do not:** learn new material; take a full mock the day before;
cram past midnight; read forum threads; bring an unapproved calculator, notes or a
phone (it voids results); leave blanks.

**During the exam:** answer everything, flag and move on, ~90 seconds per question,
if a question takes 3+ minutes guess-and-flag it, and use both breaks to reset.

## Procedure

1. Map the user's exam window to a curriculum year (2027 for February 2027 onward).
2. Choose the plan size from real availability, then state the total hours against
   300.
3. Schedule the ten topics in index order, with Ethics continuous.
4. Place mocks at ~50%, ~75%, and the final 2-3 weeks.

## Failure handling

- Runway too short to reach 300 hours at real availability -> say so and recommend
  the next window rather than inventing a shortcut.
- No verified resource year -> mark it unknown rather than recommending it.
