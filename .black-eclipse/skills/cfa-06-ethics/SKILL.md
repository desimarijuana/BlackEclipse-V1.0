## Decision rules for ambiguous questions

1. **Answer as CFA Institute wants, not as the employer or client would.** The test
   is ethical, not merely legal.
2. **Read the question's final line first** — it usually asks which action is
   *most likely* to violate, or is *least* appropriate.
3. **One Standard per question.** If two options both look wrong, choose the one
   that violates the Standard the scenario is testing.
4. **Duty hierarchy:** capital markets > clients > employer > self.
5. **Disclosure is the default remedy for conflicts** — but disclosure never cures
   a prohibited act.
6. **"I did not know" is never a defence.** "I asked my supervisor" is a defence
   only if the concern was actually followed up.
7. **Mosaic theory is permissible:** public information plus non-material
   non-public information plus independent analysis. Material non-public
   information is never tradeable.

## What to learn first

1. **Modules 1-2** (ethics and trust; the Code and Standards structure) — read once
   in week one.
2. **Modules 3-9** (the seven Standards) — one Standard per session, then
   interleave.
3. **Module 10** (application) — the practice module; do every question in it.

## Common traps

- Confusing **Standard III (clients) with Standard IV (employer)**.
- Treating **written permission as sufficient for every conflict**.
- Forgetting that **Standard VII binds candidates**, not only charterholders.
- Answering **"legally permissible"** when the question asked for "ethically
  required".
- Assuming **disclosure fixes** a conflict the Standards prohibit outright.
- Ignoring **supervisory responsibility** under Standard IV (a manager is
  responsible for subordinates' compliance).
- Assuming **mosaic theory permits trading on a material** non-public item.

## Study cadence (this is the point)

- **Start in week one.**
- **Revise weekly, minimum** — a fixed weekly Ethics session, all plan long.
- Do the end-of-module questions for every Ethics module.
- Target **75%+ before the first mock** and **80%+ by the final mock**.

- **150-200 questions**, but spread across the whole plan rather than blocked.
- Revisit **weekly** and then daily in the final two weeks.

## Dependencies

- None. Ethics is self-contained and can be studied at any point — which is
  exactly why it gets deferred.

## Failure handling

- A genuinely ambiguous scenario implicating no clear Standard -> say the question
  is under-specified rather than inventing a rule.
