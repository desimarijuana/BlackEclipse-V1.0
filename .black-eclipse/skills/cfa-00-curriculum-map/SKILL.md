# CFA Level I — Topic Index & Curriculum Map

## Purpose

The entry point for CFA Level I study. Names the ten exam topics, their weights,
their module counts, and **the order to study them in** — with Equities and
Portfolio Construction leading.

## When to use

- Starting out, or choosing what to study next.
- Any question about how a topic is weighted or what it contains.

## Why Equities and Portfolio Construction come first

They are 19-26% of the exam combined, they share the same machinery (returns,
risk, beta, CAPM, portfolio maths), and they supply the vocabulary the rest of the
exam reuses. Working them first means the return, risk and CAPM formulas are
learned once and reused in Fixed Income and Quantitative Methods instead of being
met three separate times.

**Dependency note:** Equities valuation needs the ratio work from Financial
Statement Analysis (skill 03) and the CAPM work from Quantitative Methods (skill
04). Read those sections as valuation begins rather than blocking on them — that
is the practical order even though the topic order puts Equities first.

## The 2027 changes (why older material is unusable)

1. **Quantitative Methods roughly doubled** (6-9% -> 11-14%); **Ethics was cut**
   (15-20% -> 10-15%).
2. New QM content: **estimation, simulation, portfolio optimisation, and a
   financial-data-science module covering big data, machine learning, AI and
   large language models.**
3. Equities expanded to practical valuation: **cost of equity estimation,
   multi-factor models, financial-statement forecasting in valuation, industry
   and competitive analysis, equity analyst research reports.**
4. Ethics rewritten at all levels with each Standard as its own learning module.
5. Three renames: Corporate Issuers -> **Corporate Finance**; Equity Investments
   -> **Equities**; Portfolio Management -> **Portfolio Construction**.

## Procedure

1. Identify the topic the question is about and hand off to that topic skill.
2. Use this table for weights, module counts and study order.
3. Before recommending any resource or course, check it against the 2027 changes.

## Failure handling

- Unknown exam window -> say so; do not guess a curriculum year.
- A weight claim with no curriculum year -> mark it unverified.
