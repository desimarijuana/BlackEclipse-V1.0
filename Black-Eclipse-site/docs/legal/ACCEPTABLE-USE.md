## Prohibited

- **Market abuse:** manipulation, spoofing, layering, wash trading, front-running,
  insider dealing, or any conduct that misleads a market or a participant.
- **Unauthorised access:** another person's account, broker credentials, device,
  network, or data; bypassing authentication or a broker's controls.
- **Illegal or harmful use:** fraud, money laundering, sanctions evasion, export
  control violations, harassment, or malware.
- **Circumventing safeguards:** disabling the security floor, the approval gate,
  the money guardrails, the kill switch, or the audit log; using the software to
  place an unattended live order where the product forbids it.
- **Abuse of the service:** unauthorised scraping that violates a provider's
  terms, denial-of-service, resource exhaustion, or reselling access without
  permission.
- **Misrepresentation:** presenting output as advice from `[ENTITY]`, as a
  guarantee, or as broker-confirmed when it is not.

verify material facts and orders; report a vulnerability or an unsafe behaviour.
