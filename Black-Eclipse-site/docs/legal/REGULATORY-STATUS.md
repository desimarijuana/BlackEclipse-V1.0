## What BlackEclipse is

Self-directed investment **research and order-entry software**. The user owns the
broker account, sets their own limits, and authorises each order. The broker is
the source of truth for execution. The software does not manage money, exercise
discretion, hold client assets, pool funds, or share profits/losses.

## What it is not

Not a broker, dealer, exchange, investment adviser, portfolio manager, fiduciary,
custodian, or a provider of personalised advice. It does not guarantee any
result. A connected broker or data vendor is a separate regulated entity with
its own terms.

## Why this matters

The regulatory characterisation depends on the features actually offered and the
jurisdiction: advice vs. tools, execution vs. order routing, custody, marketing,
and the AI transparency rules. This statement is only true while the product and
the marketing match it; a feature that manages, advises, or guarantees would
change the analysis.
