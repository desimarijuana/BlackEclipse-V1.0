# Complaints & Dispute Policy — DRAFT

> **DRAFT — not legal advice.** Placeholders: `[ENTITY]`, `[CONTACT]`,
> `[ADDRESS]`, `[JURISDICTION]`, `[RESPONSE DAYS]`. The channel must be real
> before launch; a policy with no inbox is not a policy.

## How to complain

Email `[CONTACT]` or use the in-product report action. Include what happened,
when, the account, and any run id or screenshot. Do not include a password or a
broker credential.

## What happens

1. We acknowledge within `[ACK DAYS]` business days.
2. We investigate and give a substantive response within `[RESPONSE DAYS]`.
3. If we need more time, we say so and give a date.
4. We record the complaint, the findings, and the remedy.

## Outcomes

A correction, a fix, a refund where the Terms allow, or a written explanation.
A security report is handled under `SECURITY.md`; a data-protection request
under `PRIVACY-NOTICE.md`.

## Escalation

If unresolved, the user may use the dispute clause in the **Terms of Service**
and, where the law allows, the regulator or ombudsman in `[JURISDICTION]`.
Nothing here removes a non-waivable right.

## Records

Complaints and outcomes are kept for `[RETENTION]` and are available for a
regulator on a lawful request.
