# Data Processing Agreement — DRAFT TEMPLATE

> **DRAFT — not legal advice.** For a hosted business customer that submits
> personal data. Counsel must complete the governing-law, transfer, and audit
> clauses. Placeholders: `[ENTITY]`, `[CUSTOMER]`, `[DPO]`, `[JURISDICTION]`,
> `[TRANSFER MECHANISM]`, `[RETENTION]`, `[AUDIT]`.

## 1. Roles

`[CUSTOMER]` is the controller of the personal data it submits; `[ENTITY]` is
the processor, acting only on documented instructions. `[ENTITY]` is the
controller of its own account, billing, and service-usage data.

## 2. Scope and purpose

Processing is limited to providing the service: hosting, auth, the agent run,
storage, support, security, and the features the customer enables. The subject
matter, duration, categories of data subjects, and data types are in Annex A.

## 3. Obligations

`[ENTITY]` will: process only on instructions (including international transfer
instructions); ensure authorised personnel are bound by confidentiality; apply
the security measures in Annex B; assist with data-subject requests and DPIAs
to the extent the law requires; notify a personal-data breach without undue
delay; and delete or return data on termination, subject to legal retention.

## 4. Subprocessors

The current list is `SUBPROCESSORS.md`. `[ENTITY]` gives notice of a new
subprocessor and a reasonable objection window. The customer may object on
data-protection grounds; if unresolved, it may terminate the affected service.

## 5. International transfers

`[TRANSFER MECHANISM]` (for example an adequacy decision or standard
contractual clauses), with supplementary measures where needed.

## 6. Audit

Once per `[PERIOD]`, on reasonable notice, `[ENTITY]` answers a security
questionnaire and provides the current audit evidence. On-site audit
`[AUDIT]`.

## 7. Liability and term

The DPA follows the main agreement's liability and term, except where the law
requires otherwise. Annexes A (processing details), B (security measures), and
C (transfer mechanism) are part of this DPA.
