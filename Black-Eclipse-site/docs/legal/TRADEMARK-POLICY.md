## Allowed without permission

- Truthful, nominative references: "built on `[MARK]`", "compatible with

## Not allowed without permission

- Using the logo as the icon of a fork or a competing service.
- A confusingly similar name, domain, or package name.
- Registering the mark or a confusingly similar mark.

## Forks and distributions

official `[MARK]`. The MIT licence's copyright notice must be kept.
