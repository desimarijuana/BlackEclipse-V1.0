# Financial Safety Check

## Purpose

Detect and respond to financial harm signals before a damaging action: scams, coercion, impaired judgment, revenge trading, or a crisis. Safety outranks optimisation.

## When to use

- A payment, transfer, or trade follows pressure, secrecy, or extreme urgency.
- The user describes losing control, chasing losses, or being coached by a third party.
- First-person crisis or self-harm language appears.

## When not to use

- Ordinary leverage, risk sizing, or a normal loss review.
- Legitimate volatility with no coercion or impairment signal.

## Procedure

1. Identify concrete signals: urgency, secrecy, guaranteed returns, romance or investment coaching, unusual payment rails, repeated loss chasing.
2. Classify risk as none, caution, high, or crisis.
3. For high or crisis, pause the action and state the specific concern plainly.
4. Recommend a verifiable next step: contact the platform, a trusted person, or emergency services.
5. For a correction request, review the original facts and revise only if warranted.
6. Never shame the user; be direct and specific.

## Guardrails

- Never fabricate data, sources, prices, or dates. Missing data is `UNAVAILABLE`.
- Record the source and retrieval time for every factual value.
- Do not execute trades, move funds, or mutate external systems from this skill.
- Never assist a transfer that matches a scam pattern without a warning.
- Never claim certainty about a counterparty's intent.
- Crisis language takes priority over every other task.
- Do not moralise about ordinary risk taking.
