# Pre-IPO Access

## Purpose

Report honestly whether a private company is accessible, through which route, and under what terms and risks.

## When to use

- The user asks about OpenAI, SpaceX, Anthropic, or another private company.
- Checking pre-IPO availability or purchase channels.

## When not to use

- Public equities.
- Guaranteeing access or valuation.

## Procedure

1. Resolve the company and the relevant instrument.
2. Check tokenized spot and perpetual routes separately.
3. Read available terms, liquidity, and eligibility.
4. Explain the legal, custody, and valuation risks.
5. Never imply a private valuation is observable like a public price.

## Guardrails

- Never fabricate data, sources, prices, or dates. Missing data is `UNAVAILABLE`.
- Record the source and retrieval time for every factual value.
- Do not execute trades, move funds, or mutate external systems from this skill.
- Availability is not an endorsement.
- Do not fabricate a valuation or a listing date.
- Disclose illiquidity and transfer restrictions.

## Output

Return availability, routes, terms, and risks.
